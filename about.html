Our Mission

Pure air, every breath.

Dhaka and Chattogram consistently rank among the cities with the worst air quality globally. With rapid urbanization and the health awareness of the post-pandemic era, families are increasingly focusing on respiratory health—especially for young children and elderly members.

Nirmol was born out of a critical market gap. The current indoor air market is split: high-quality imported products are too expensive, while low-cost, unbranded imports lack reliability and after-sales support.

We engineered a solution specifically designed to handle high concentrations of PM2.5 and dust. By providing affordable pricing, flexible EMI financing, and strong local warranties, Nirmol ensures that pure air is no longer a luxury, but an accessible right for every home in Bangladesh.
